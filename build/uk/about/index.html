About the toolbox

Circular Economy tools for your business

The Circular Economy Toolbox has been developed under EU4Green Recovery East - regional EU-funded programme to support the Eastern Partnership (EaP) countries in their efforts to make environmental policies and cooperation more effective and more supportive of countries development goals. The Circular Economy Toolbox helps small and medium-sized enterprises across the Eastern Partnership countries identify, understand, and apply digital resources that support their transition to circular economy practices. It is designed for small-medium enterprises (SMEs) at any stage of that journey, from those just starting to explore circularity to those ready to evaluate, implement and monitor circular strategies within their operations.

The toolbox brings together a range of tools, including interactive digital tools such as calculators, canvases, and self-assessment platforms, databases and networks that map infrastructure and connect users to relevant providers, and guides and methodologies that offer support. Each resource is selected and organised according to its relevance to where SMEs and the specific sectors they work in.

Resources are reviewed periodically to confirm that links remain active, information stays accurate, and content continues to reflect current practice. As new tools, methodologies, and case examples become available, they are added to keep the catalogue relevant for small-medium enterprises SMEs navigating their circular economy transition.
